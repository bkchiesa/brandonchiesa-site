Valmozzola

San Siro and San Martino, the home villages of the Chiesa, Galli, Ferretti and Manfredi families, as the parish and civil records describe them, with photos from Brandon’s visit in June 2026.

This page uses only what the family’s records and the research notes say. Each statement keeps its rating; anything uncertain is marked. It is not a general history of the comune.

Proven a record says so · Probable index or strong match · Hypothesis a working idea, unproven · Family-provided Brandon’s photos

San Siro

San Siro is the village of the Chiesa line. Pietro Chiesa was baptised at the church of San Siro on 19 October 1789, son of Donnino Chiesa and Margarita Coduri Proven. In 1815 he married Giovanna Costa, aged 15; the wedding was celebrated at San Giacomo di Branzone Proven. Source: baptism register DGS 7958601; 1815 marriage act

The 1803 stato d’anime, a parish household census, lists the Chiesa and Costa households Proven. Domenico Chiesa was baptised at the San Siro church on 23 June 1828, which matches his civil birth entry Proven. Source: DGS 7958602; Nati 1828 atto 32

Uncertain: the 18th-century baptisms name the parish of San Giacomo, Branzone. It is not confirmed that the San Siro church in the photos is the church those records mean, and Pietro’s baptism may have been at a different parish church Hypothesis.

Domenico’s sons Giovanni (John) and Giuseppe (Joseph) were born at San Siro, at house nos. 22 and 10 Proven. Both later left for New York. Source: Nati 1881 atto 55; Nati 1886 atto 12

San Martino

San Martino, with its church of San Martino Vescovo, is the village of the maternal lines. Its baptism books from the 1770s record the Galli, Ferretti and Manfredi families: children of Alfonso Galli from 1772, of Eugenio and Antonia Ferretti from 1767, and of Camillo Manfredi and Catharina Dalla Tana Proven. Source: San Martino baptisms, DGS 7958653 (Book A)

Martina Galli was born in San Martino in 1859 Proven, and her daughter Marianna Ferretti was born at San Martino no. 6 in 1885 Proven. Martina sailed in June 1906 with her daughter Veronica. Marianna followed in October 1910, travelling in a group from Valmozzola Probable. Source: Nati 1859 atto 6; Nati 1885 atto 34; Ellis Island manifests

Uncertain: the brides Anastasia Conti and Veronica Bergenti (also read Borgonti) were not baptised in San Martino. They probably came from the neighbouring parish of Pieve di Gusaliggio Hypothesis, whose register has not yet been read.

The marriage record, Manhattan 1915

Giuseppe Chiesa and Marianna Ferretti, both from Valmozzola, married in New York on 21 October 1915 at the church of Our Lady of Pompei, Manhattan Proven. Source: NYC Certificate and Record of Marriage no. 24034

The war memorial at San Siro

A marble plaque dated 1 March 1925 hangs on the front wall of the San Siro parish church. It names three men: Cap. Magg. Domenico Chiesa and the soldiers Agostino Chiesa and Giacomo Ferrari. Proven (Brandon’s photo of the plaque)

All three appear in the Albo d’oro, Italy’s roll of the war dead Probable (index entries):

  1. CHIESA Domenico di Giuseppe, b. 16 Apr 1891 Valmozzola; Caporale (plaque: cap. magg.); 9th Infantry Rgt; d. 20 Nov 1915 at Ospedaletto da campo n. 80 of wounds received in combat; buried Redipuglia; vol. VIII p. 219 n. 26 Albo d’oro ↗
  2. CHIESA Agostino di Pietro, b. 14 Jul 1895 Valmozzola; Soldato; 4th Bersaglieri Rgt; d. 17 Nov 1917 in Rome of illness; vol. VIII p. 219 n. 23 Albo d’oro ↗
  3. FERRARI Giacomo di Antonio, b. 19 Nov 1882 Valmozzola; Soldato; 6th Alpini Rgt; d. 24 Feb 1918 as a prisoner of war, of illness; buried Milovice military cemetery (Czech Rep.); vol. VIII p. 297 n. 20 Albo d’oro ↗

Uncertain: their kinship to Brandon’s line is unproven; they were probably cousins Hypothesis. Their fathers (Giuseppe Chiesa fl. 1891, Pietro Chiesa fl. 1895, Antonio Ferrari fl. 1882) match no person in the tree. All three are in the Albo as Valmozzola-born, so the comune memorial catalogue entry (0800430275), whose 'San Siro 1915-1918' list differs, is probably incomplete or a different list.
